03 · Lash lift aftercare

Protect
the lift.

Your first 48 hours set the tone. Follow these studio-approved steps to keep the curl polished and the natural lash healthy.

01

Keep lashes dry

First 24 hours

Give the final nourishing step time to fully penetrate the lash, inside and out.

02

Skip heat and steam

First 48 hours

Avoid saunas and steam rooms. Excessive heat can affect the shape and longevity of the lift.

03

Choose oil-free

Every day

Use an oil-free makeup remover. Oil can break down the lift; a gentle oil-free mousse is ideal.

04

Brush to reset

When wet

Use a clean spoolie while lashes are damp to keep the lift sitting neatly.

05

No waterproof mascara

Protect the result

Waterproof formulas demand extra rubbing during removal, which can lead to breakage.

06

Retire the curler

No clamping

Lash curlers can cause breakage on lifted or natural lashes.
